Kinetic plasma research at scale

Test problems from the ECsim papers, and the plasma regimes the code is intended for.

Kinetic plasma physics

PIC methods follow non-equilibrium particle distributions and kinetic effects that fluid models cannot capture. ECsim is fully electromagnetic and, being semi-implicit, is unconditionally stable in time, so the time step is set by the physics of interest rather than by a stability limit.

Space and astrophysical plasmas

ECsim-X targets space plasma problems such as collisionless plasma environments, shocks, and magnetic reconnection. Suitability for a particular configuration depends on dimensionality, boundary conditions, scale separation, and the physics supported by the code.

Electromagnetic simulations

Particles are coupled to electric and magnetic fields through a linear Maxwell solve on a Cartesian grid, with the mover treating the magnetic rotation exactly through the α tensor. Verify supported boundary conditions and geometries in the distribution before planning a study.
