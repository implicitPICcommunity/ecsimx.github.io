Respectful scientific collaboration

Code of Conduct

This page describes the standards expected in project-related communication, scientific discussion, and collaboration around ECsim-X.

Participants are expected to help maintain a respectful, evidence-based, and constructive environment. Scientific disagreement is welcome when it is expressed professionally and focused on methods, evidence, and results.

Expected behavior

  • Communicate respectfully and professionally.
  • Discuss scientific claims using evidence, reproducible observations, and clear reasoning.
  • Welcome good-faith questions and acknowledge uncertainty or limitations.
  • Give appropriate credit for ideas, code, data, and scientific work.
  • Provide actionable, relevant feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
  • Respect privacy and do not publish private correspondence or personal information without permission.

Unacceptable behavior

  • Harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
  • Deliberate disruption of technical or scientific discussions.
  • Misrepresenting another person's work or knowingly presenting unsupported claims as established facts.
  • Sharing private information without consent.
  • Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

Scope

These expectations apply to interactions specifically related to ECsim-X, including project communication channels, scientific discussions, collaboration, and events organized by the project team.

Raising a concern

Maintainer contact needed Add a verified contact for reporting conduct concerns and explain how reports will be reviewed. Until a contact is published, no reporting channel or enforcement process is implied. Concerns should be handled as confidentially as practical and reviewed fairly.

Response

Project maintainers should review reported concerns impartially, consider the available information, and communicate any response through an appropriate channel. Corrective action should be proportionate to the behavior and consistent with applicable project policies.

Status: This is a proposed project policy for review by the ECsim-X maintainers. It should be approved and adapted by the project team before being presented as an officially adopted policy.
