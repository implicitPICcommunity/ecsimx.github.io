Scientific use cases

A framework for kinetic plasma research

Particle-in-Cell simulations are used to study kinetic plasma phenomena and electromagnetic particle dynamics. The areas below are potential application domains; no specific ECsim-X application result or publication was supplied for verification.

Kinetic plasma physics

PIC methods can represent non-equilibrium particle distributions and kinetic effects that are difficult to capture with fluid-only descriptions. Possible study areas include wave-particle interactions, plasma instabilities, and particle acceleration, depending on the model and available physics.

Space and astrophysical plasmas

Kinetic simulations are used in research on collisionless plasma environments, shocks, and magnetic reconnection. Application suitability depends on dimensionality, boundary conditions, scale separation, and the physical models supported by the code.

Electromagnetic simulations

Electromagnetic PIC approaches couple charged-particle motion to electric and magnetic fields. The available field equations, boundary conditions, geometry, and diagnostics should be documented from ECsim-X source code before claiming support for a particular configuration.
